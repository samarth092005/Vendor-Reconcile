Executive Summary

Vendor Reconcile is a robust, enterprise-grade execution platform built on the SAP Cloud Application Programming (CAP) framework and Node.js. It is specifically engineered to eliminate manual financial discrepancies by acting as a highly automated verification layer between internal procurement workflows and external vendor billing.

The system has achieved complete live integration with the SAP S/4HANA (S21) ERP system, actively pulling Purchase Orders, Goods Receipts, and Vendor Master Data natively via secure OData APIs. By orchestrating a mathematically rigorous 3-Way Matching Engine, the platform empowers Accounts Payable teams to achieve touchless processing for clear invoices while providing a dedicated dispute management portal for exceptions.
1. CORE PROJECT PILLARS & VALUE PROPOSITION

✓ End-to-End P2P Governance

Complete lifecycle management from Purchase Requisition creation to UTR payment clearing, governed by strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across five distinct operational personas.

✓ Automated Invoice Matching

✓ Seamless SAP S21 Sync

3. LIVE SAP INTEGRATION MAPPING (S21 DATA FLOW)
The application is rigorously connected to the live SAP S21 ERP system. The custom s21-connector.js orchestrates synchronization, guaranteeing that local data precisely mirrors SAP business reality.
SAP Module Integration Endpoint Engineered Implementation Details
Purchase Orders (ME23N) API_PURCHASEORDER_PROCESS_SRV/A_PurchaseOrder Extracts expected pricing, delivery terms, and PO line items. Recent Upgrade: Dynamically parses the PO payload to extract the true AddressName for suppliers, replacing numeric ID fallbacks.
Supplier Invoices (MIRO) API_SUPPLIERINVOICE_PROCESS_SRV/A_SupplierInvoice Ingests supplier billing data. Recent Upgrade: Since the SAP invoice item endpoint lacks material codes, the engine now actively cross-references the PO database to map true material IDs (e.g., 10314) to invoice lines.
Goods Receipts (MIGO) API_MATERIAL_DOCUMENT_SRV/A_MaterialDocumentItem Extracts physical warehouse intake quantities, allowing the engine to calculate whether a vendor is billing for unreceived freight (Uninvoiced GR tracking).
4. THE AUTOMATED 3-WAY MATCHING ENGINE
At the heart of the platform is the custom Node.js verification engine (matching-engine.js). It evaluates aggregated SAP data against vendor uploads to determine financial clearance.

✓ CLEARED MATCHED / 3-WAY-CLEAR

Logic: The system successfully locates the PO in SAP, verifies the invoiced price is within 2% tolerance of the expected PO rate, and ensures the invoiced quantity does not exceed the warehouse GR logged quantity.

Action: Approved for automated MIRO processing and UTR payment generation.

⚠ DISPUTE UNMATCHED / PRICE-DIFF

Logic: The expected PO price differs from the Vendor's billed amount by more than the strict organizational threshold.

Action: Automatically generates an Exception ticket allocated to the Finance Exec queue.

⚠ DISPUTE UNMATCHED / QTY-EXCEEDS-GR

Logic: The vendor is attempting to bill for a higher volume of material than what was physically received and scanned by the GR Specialist.

Action: Routed to Procurement for vendor credit-note requests.

✖ CRITICAL UNMATCHED / PO-NOT-FOUND

Logic: A vendor invoice was uploaded, but the engine cannot locate any active Purchase Order reference within the synced SAP database.

Action: Immediate AP Manager intervention required to block fraudulent billing.

5. EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD & KPI INTELLIGENCE
The dashboard (Cockpit.controller.js & kpi-handler.js) mathematically aggregates the outcomes of the matching engine in real-time, yielding critical operational insights.

Missing in File

Actively queries the GrirSnapshot to locate open SAP Goods Receipts that have no corresponding vendor invoice attached to them yet.

Duplicates

Scans VendorInvoiceHeaders dynamically to catch overlapping, identical invoice submissions from a single vendor to prevent double-billing.

High-Value Exceptions

Continuously monitors MatchResults to flag any singular invoice dispute where the financial variance exceeds ₹50,000.

Vendor Risk Mapping

Compiles a live ledger ranking vendors by the volume of their active disputes (e.g., Endurance Components, Stark Industry).

6. ROLE-BASED OPERATIONAL WORKFLOWS
The system enforces strict RBAC (Role-Based Access Control) to govern the reconciliation journey. Distinct personas operate within their isolated UI scopes.
PR
PR Indenter
Initiates the procurement cycle by raising Purchase Requisitions for required raw materials or services within SAP.
PO
PO Officer
Audits requisitions, approves pricing, converts PRs to official Purchase Orders, and dispatches them to external suppliers.
VEN
Vendor
Logs into a restricted self-service portal to view awarded POs, mark dispatch statuses, and upload digital invoice copies.
GR
GR Specialist
Manages physical logistics, recording MIGO goods receipts directly reflecting actual warehouse intake.
FIN
Finance Exec
Reviews matching engine exceptions, negotiates disputes, overrides tolerances, and clears invoices for UTR execution.
7. DATA GOVERNANCE & SCHEMA ARCHITECTURE
To isolate transactional telemetry without polluting the SAP Master Data environment, the CAP Application utilizes a structured internal database schema comprising over 10 interconnected entities.
Core Entity (DB) Purpose & Relational Mapping
VendorMaster Stores normalized supplier profiles. Synced dynamically via SAP PO endpoints to capture accurate businessPartnerName, avoiding raw numeric ID leakage in UI displays.
PurchaseOrders Mirrors active SAP ME23N records. Contains granular line-item details (Qty, Amount, Material ID) serving as the baseline truth for the Matching Engine.
VendorInvoiceHeaders & Items Houses ingested supplier billing documents (either synced from SAP MIRO or uploaded via portal). Line items cross-reference PurchaseOrders to derive true expected materials.
MatchResults The immutable audit ledger written exclusively by the Matching Engine. Links an Invoice Item to a PO Item and statically records the evaluated outcomeCode, absolute amountDelta, and rule applied.
Exceptions Spawns conditionally when a MatchResult fails verification (e.g. PRICE-DIFF). Tracks resolution status (OPEN/RESOLVED), SLA aging, and assigns priority (P1/P2/P3) for Finance AP queues.
8. STRATEGIC ROADMAP & NEXT PHASES

1. Native OCR Ingestion Pipeline

Integration with Microsoft/Grok optical character recognition to fully automate the Data Intake process. Unstructured vendor email PDFs will be autonomously scraped, validated, and inserted into the VendorInvoiceHeaders table without human keystrokes.

2. Automated UTR Settlement (Outbound)

Enhancing the outbound proxy connectors to actively push MATCHED/3-WAY-CLEAR invoices back into SAP F110 (Automatic Payment Run). This will close the loop, automatically clearing payables and echoing the final Bank UTR numbers back to the Vendor UI.
